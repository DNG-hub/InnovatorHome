# Blog artwork direction

Every new blog article receives its own original illustration. Topic artwork establishes the painting style, palette, and visual language; the article illustration depicts that article’s specific theme or central discovery.

Generate the final composition for a 3.5:1 landscape frame. Keep the primary subject and meaningful detail within that wide frame. Do not create a taller composition and rely on a centered crop to make it fit.

The site retains its 3.5:1 cover frame as a defensive display rule. Before accepting new art, verify that the original composition retains its intended narrative when viewed at that ratio.

Existing artwork is not to be regenerated solely to meet this rule.
