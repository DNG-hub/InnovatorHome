# VALESKA evolution article dating

Every VALESKA evolution article uses the date of its primary historical evidence:

- an original document creation date;
- a repository commit date; or
- a HECL decision-record creation date.

The article metadata records the evidence source. The narrative must be written from the knowledge available at that date. It may identify an earlier historical source, but it must not present later technology, decisions, or partnerships as though they were known at that time.

Write the title and narrative in the contemporaneous author voice: describe the work, questions, decisions, and expectations as current on the article's date. A clearly labeled historical-basis note may identify the evidence used to date the entry, but must not turn the article into a retrospective account.

The first repository-era entry is dated August 8, 2025, the initial VALESKA repository commit (`be8a50a`) and initial software-design document. Earlier value-stream and VSKA materials support separately dated historical entries.
