# Blog publication workflow

1. Draft and review the English source article, including its evidence date, metadata, and article-specific artwork.
2. Dave's request to begin the next English blog finalizes the preceding English article. At that point, create and publish its reviewed Spanish and Portuguese renditions before drafting the new English article.
3. Keep Spanish and Portuguese versions of the current English article as unpublished working drafts until Dave moves to the next article. Do not revise or publish them while English is still being corrected.
4. Preserve the source article's historical date, evidence record, topic, tags, and cover artwork across the translations. Translate the title, description, article text, image alternative text, and the human-readable historical note.
5. Before choosing the next English article after an implementation milestone, inspect subsequent commits, tests, and operational records for actual-practice issues or lessons. Record the difference between planned, tested, and observed behavior in the next article's evidence base.
6. Save all finalized language records to the content database, export the snapshot, build the site, and verify the published routes and read-only agent catalog.

An English source revision change makes its translations stale until they are reviewed and updated against that revision.
