# Blog publication workflow

1. Draft and review the English source article, including its evidence date, metadata, and article-specific artwork.
2. When Dave requests the next blog, treat the preceding English article as final and create reviewed Spanish and Portuguese renditions before drafting the next English article.
3. Preserve the source article's historical date, evidence record, topic, tags, and cover artwork across the translations. Translate the title, description, article text, image alternative text, and the human-readable historical note.
4. Save all three language records to the content database, export the snapshot, build the site, and verify the published routes and read-only agent catalog.

An English source revision change makes its translations stale until they are reviewed and updated against that revision.
